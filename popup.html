Chart & layout
v1.13.0

Default position is now near the upper-right chart area, like your TradingView placement.

Phase colors

Default: first 33% stays green. Then it blends toward yellow, then gradually toward red near candle close.

Text & market context

Auto round rules: BTC = 500 (5,000 major), Gold = 10 (100 major), US Oil = 0.1 (0.5 / whole-number levels become major). Only the nearest level above and below are shown, with the live distance from current price in parentheses. Major levels use bold text.

Clock is a plain numerical clock with no icon/seconds. TradingView cards use live multi-timeframe OHLC when the scanner supports the chart symbol. Exness uses the active chart OHLC immediately and tracks other timeframes; unverified candles stay neutral instead of showing a false green/red signal.

Panel appearance
Ring & automatic panel size

The panel auto-sizes from the ring, large countdown/clock and enabled timeframe cards. Cards use a clearer full-width horizontal candle preview with larger timeframe/countdown text; only the candle body/wick proportions change with OHLC.

Behavior

Only use the alignment offset when broker candle boundaries differ from standard UTC boundaries.

Candle cards & selection

Click a candle card to switch the chart. The selected highlight follows the detected chart interval. Unsupported intervals must be selected from the chart toolbar.
